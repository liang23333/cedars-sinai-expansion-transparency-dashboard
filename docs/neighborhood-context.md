**Site:** Cedars-Sinai Medical Center Expansion

## 1. Geographic & Urban Setting

The Cedars-Sinai Medical Center campus is located in a vibrant, high-density sector of West Los Angeles, precisely on the border of the **Beverly Grove** neighborhood of Los Angeles and the municipality of **West Hollywood**, just east of the City of **Beverly Hills**. 

The main campus spans across several city blocks framed by primary arterial thoroughfares:
- **Beverly Boulevard (North):** Major east-west commercial corridor connecting Downtown LA to Beverly Hills.
- **West 3rd Street (South):** Active pedestrian, boutique retail, and dining corridor.
- **South San Vicente Boulevard (East):** Major diagonal arterial carrying heavy commuter traffic between Midtown and West Hollywood.
- **North Robertson Boulevard (West):** High-end commercial, design district, and dining destination.
- **Internal Campus Streets:** Gracie Allen Drive and George Burns Road provide internal circulatory access for ambulances, patient drop-offs, and hospital staff.

## 2. Healthcare & Clinical Ecosystem

Cedars-Sinai is one of the largest non-profit academic healthcare complexes in the western United States. The surrounding area features a dense cluster of specialized clinical services:

| Facility Name | Address / Location | Description & Relationship to Project |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cedars-Sinai Main Hospital & North Tower** | 8700 Beverly Blvd | Central inpatient care, acute surgeries, Level I Trauma Center |
| **East Medical Office Tower** | 8631 W 3rd St (OSM `661062919`) | Outpatient specialty clinics, physician offices, diagnostics |
| **Advanced Health Sciences Pavilion** | San Vicente Blvd & Gracie Allen Dr | Outpatient surgery, regenerative medicine, neurosciences |
| **Saperstein Critical Care Tower** | Gracie Allen Dr | Dedicated ICU, coronary care, and organ transplant facilities |
| **Saban Community Clinic** | 8405 Beverly Blvd | Community partner providing safety-net medical & dental care |
| **Lasky Clinic** | Charleville Blvd / Beverly Hills | Nearby ambulatory surgical center and outpatient care |

### Clinical Protection Protocols During Expansion
- **Vibration Isolation:** Continuous seismic and vibration sensors placed on shared structural walls with critical care units to protect sensitive microsurgical procedures and MRI imaging.
- **Air Intake Safeguards:** Construction dust barriers and directional exhaust ventilation positioned away from HVAC intake louvers on the North Tower and Saperstein Pavilion.

## 5. Parking Infrastructure & Traffic Mitigation

Parking in the Beverly Grove / West Hollywood zone is heavily impacted. The expansion project utilizes strict parking and traffic management rules:

### Dedicated Parking Structures
- **North Parking Structure:** Beverly Boulevard (OSM Way `534711304`, lat: `34.0763812`, lon: `-118.3801190`) — Designated patient and visitor parking.
- **South Sherbourne Parking Structure:** South Sherbourne Dr (OSM Way `31439503`, lat: `34.0740994`, lon: `-118.3799516`) — Staff and physician parking.
- **Beverly Center Garages:** Auxiliary contracted parking for construction management teams and trade contractors.

### Contractor Traffic Rules
- **Street Parking Prohibition:** Subcontractors are contractually barred from parking on residential streets in Beverly Grove and West Hollywood. Violations result in immediate badge revocation.
- **Staging & Haul Routes:** Heavy machinery and concrete mixers must ingress via San Vicente Blvd and La Cienega Blvd, strictly avoiding residential side streets.

## 6. Public Transit & Mobility Network

The Cedars-Sinai campus is one of the most transit-connected medical hubs in Southern California:

### On-Site Transit Stops
1. **Gracie Allen Dr & George Burns Rd (OSM Node `10000721666`):** Located right in the heart of the medical campus.
2. **San Vicente Blvd & Gracie Allen Dr (OSM Node `8171938071`):** Serves eastern campus access and Beverly Center.
3. **San Vicente Blvd & Beverly Blvd (OSM Node `8171938070`):** Major bus transfer junction.

### Transit Lines
- **Metro Bus Line 14/37:** Operates along Beverly Boulevard directly connecting West Hollywood, Koreatown, and Downtown Los Angeles.
- **Metro Bus Line 105:** Operates along La Cienega Boulevard connecting West Hollywood to South Los Angeles.
- **Metro Bus Line 30/330:** Connects San Vicente Boulevard with Pico-Union and Downtown LA.
- **City of West Hollywood Cityline:** Free local shuttle connecting the campus to Santa Monica Blvd and the Sunset Strip.
- **Metro D Line (Purple Line Extension):** Future subway connection at Wilshire/La Cienega station (~0.8 miles south), which will provide rapid transit access to UCLA/Westwood and Downtown LA.

### Pedestrian & Bicycle Safety
- Continuous covered pedestrian scaffolds along Beverly Blvd and Gracie Allen Dr.
- High-visibility crosswalks with audible pedestrian signals at all major campus intersections.
- Micro-mobility stations (Metro Bike Share) located on Gracie Allen Dr.
