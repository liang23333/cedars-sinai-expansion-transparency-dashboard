# Expansion Phasing & Operational Blueprint

**Project:** Cedars-Sinai Medical Center Expansion  
**Site:** 8700 Beverly Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90048  
**Governing Authorities:** California Department of Public Health (CDPH), Department of Health Care Access and Information (HCAI / formerly OSHPD), City of Los Angeles Building & Safety (LADBS), City of West Hollywood.

## Phase 1: Foundation Completion
- **Tracking Issue:** [Issue #1](https://github.com/liang23333/cedars-sinai-expansion-transparency-dashboard/issues/1)
- **Target Duration:** Months 1 – 9
- **Core Scope:**
  - Site mobilization, utility disconnection, and rerouting of underground hospital conduits.
  - Soldier pile and lagging shoring wall installation along Beverly Blvd and Gracie Allen Dr.
  - Deep excavation of approximately 150,000 cubic yards of soil.
  - Installation of friction piles and HCAI Level 1 seismic base isolation pedestals.
  - Continuous 12,000 cu yd mass concrete foundation mat pour.
- **Weather Mitigation Key Actions:**
  - Continuous pit dewatering and automated water testing before discharge.
  - Concrete cooling protocols during warm periods to mitigate hydration heat.
  - PM10 dust suppression cannons during earthmoving operations.
- **Neighborhood Coordination:**
  - Heavy haul truck routing strictly confined to San Vicente Blvd and La Cienega Blvd.
  - Acoustic and seismic vibration dampening against adjacent inpatient wards.

---

## Phase 2: Structural Framing
- **Tracking Issue:** [Issue #2](https://github.com/liang23333/cedars-sinai-expansion-transparency-dashboard/issues/2)
- **Target Duration:** Months 10 – 20
- **Core Scope:**
  - Erection of twin heavy-lift tower cranes with FAA night-lighting clearance.
  - Structural steel framing, moment-frame welding, and installation of buckling-restrained braces (BRBs).
  - Placement of metal decking and elevated concrete floor slabs.
  - Exterior facade bracket and curtain wall support attachment.
  - Structural Topping Out milestone celebration.
- **Weather Mitigation Key Actions:**
  - Automated wind anemometers on crane booms triggering work suspension at >30 mph gusts.
  - Cal/OSHA high heat protocols for ironworkers on open steel decks.
  - Lightning detection system with 10-mile safety radius.
- **Neighborhood Coordination:**
  - Crane swing limiting sensors to prevent boom transit over Beverly Center or public roadways.
  - Overhead heavy-duty protective pedestrian tunnels along Beverly Blvd.

---

## Phase 3: Interior Fit-Out
- **Tracking Issue:** [Issue #3](https://github.com/liang23333/cedars-sinai-expansion-transparency-dashboard/issues/3)
- **Target Duration:** Months 21 – 32
- **Core Scope:**
  - Exterior glass curtain wall and roofing dry-in certification.
  - MEP installation: central plant chillers, emergency electrical buses, cleanroom air handlers.
  - Medical gas piping (oxygen, vacuum, nitrous oxide) and pneumatic tube transport network.
  - Lead-lined radiological rooms (MRI, CT, PET scans) and sterile operating suites.
  - Interior framing, drywall, acoustic baffles, and anti-microbial wall coatings.
- **Weather Mitigation Key Actions:**
  - Enclosed climate control to combat 70%+ coastal marine layer humidity.
  - Thermal load balancing under ambient summer conditions.
- **Neighborhood Coordination:**
  - Contractor parking assigned to Cedars P1/P4 decks; strict neighborhood street parking ban.
  - Interior loading docks on Alden Drive utilized for off-peak deliveries.

---

## Phase 4: Operational Readiness & Licensing
- **Tracking Issue:** [Issue #4](https://github.com/liang23333/cedars-sinai-expansion-transparency-dashboard/issues/4)
- **Target Duration:** Months 33 – 38
- **Core Scope:**
  - Heavy clinical equipment installation and factory calibration.
  - Life-safety commissioning (smoke evacuation, fire suppression, diesel generator transfer).
  - Epic EHR, medical telemetry, and hospital IT infrastructure verification.
  - CDPH state licensing inspection and HCAI final occupancy approval.
  - Multi-day simulated patient transfer and mass-casualty disaster response drills.
  - Public ribbon-cutting and community transparency showcase.
- **Weather Mitigation Key Actions:**
  - 96-hour emergency power microgrid testing under simulated high-heat power grid brownout conditions.
  - Helipad de-icing and thunderstorm flight approach verification.
  - Multi-stage MERV 16 air filtration validation during regional wildfire smoke events.
- **Neighborhood Coordination:**
  - Emergency vehicle ingress/egress calibration with West Hollywood and LAPD traffic units.
  - Community open house tours and final project impact reporting.
