## 🗺️ Vicinity Profile & Neighborhood Context

The expansion site occupies a high-density urban nexus bounded by Beverly Boulevard to the north, San Vicente Boulevard to the east, West 3rd Street to the south, and North Robertson Boulevard to the west. A comprehensive OpenStreetMap geospatial audit identified the key surrounding infrastructure:

- **Clinical & Healthcare Ecosystem:** Directly integrated with the Cedars-Sinai campus, including the East Medical Office Tower (8631 W 3rd St), Saperstein Critical Care Center, Advanced Health Sciences Pavilion, and regional clinics such as Lasky Clinic and Saban Community Clinic.
- **Pharmacies & Medical Supplies:** Immediate proximity to CVS Pharmacy on Beverly Blvd (~400m east), Cedars-Sinai Outpatient Pharmacy, and specialized clinical compounding facilities.
- **Dining & Commercial Amenities:** Across San Vicente Blvd sits the landmark Beverly Center (8500 Beverly Blvd) housing dining establishments (Cal Mare, Eggslut, Commissary, For The Win) and retail; the pedestrian-friendly West 3rd Street and Robertson Boulevard corridors provide diverse neighborhood food options.
- **Parking Infrastructure:** Campus-dedicated parking structures including North Parking (Beverly Blvd, OSM `534711304`) and South Sherbourne Parking (OSM `31439503`), augmented by Beverly Center multi-level garages. Subcontractor logistics plans prohibit street parking impact on adjacent Beverly Grove residential streets.
- **Transit & Multimodal Access:** On-site transit nodes at Gracie Allen Dr & George Burns Rd (OSM `10000721666`), San Vicente Blvd & Gracie Allen Dr (OSM `8171938071`), and San Vicente Blvd & Beverly Blvd (OSM `8171938070`). Served by Metro Lines 14/37, 105, 30/330, and convenient connections to the future Metro D Line (Purple Line Extension) Wilshire/La Cienega station.

## 🏗️ Project Milestones & Phase Tracking

The expansion unfolds in four sequential opening phases. Track real-time progress and contribute community feedback via our milestone issues:

1. [**Issue #1 — Milestone Phase 1: Foundation Completion**](https://github.com/liang23333/cedars-sinai-expansion-transparency-dashboard/issues/1)  
   *Mass excavation, shoring walls, HCAI Level 1 seismic base isolators, and mass concrete mat foundation.*
2. [**Issue #2 — Milestone Phase 2: Structural Framing**](https://github.com/liang23333/cedars-sinai-expansion-transparency-dashboard/issues/2)  
   *Superstructure steel erection, seismic dampers, composite decking, tower crane safety protocols, and topping out.*
3. [**Issue #3 — Milestone Phase 3: Interior Fit-Out**](https://github.com/liang23333/cedars-sinai-expansion-transparency-dashboard/issues/3)  
   *Building envelope dry-in, MEP systems, sterile airflow, medical gas infrastructure, and diagnostic imaging suites.*
4. [**Issue #4 — Milestone Phase 4: Operational Readiness & Licensing**](https://github.com/liang23333/cedars-sinai-expansion-transparency-dashboard/issues/4)  
   *Clinical systems commissioning, CDPH/HCAI life-safety surveys, grid resilience testing, and emergency vehicle routing.*
